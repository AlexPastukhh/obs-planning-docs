# Landscape Research And Evolution Guidance

Responsibility ID: `RK.LANDSCAPE-RESEARCH`

Landscape Analysis builds a bounded current understanding of a selected knowledge landscape from available Bank knowledge and other accepted Sources.

The useful result may be historical, comparative, structural, evolutionary, distributional or mixed. Do not force a fixed analytical checklist.

> Semantic Owner Dependencies
> - `EXTENDS` [Knowledge Basis Contract](../../../idtspe-core/knowledge-bases/KNOWLEDGE-BASIS-CONTRACT.md#knowledge-basis-contract) — `KNOWLEDGE.BASIS`

## Adaptive Inquiry Principle

```text
current Landscape concern
→ inspect available evidence
→ surface useful questions
→ pursue the questions most likely to improve the current understanding
→ re-evaluate after material findings
→ stop when additional depth is unlikely to materially improve the useful result
```

A large possible question space is not a requirement to exhaust it.

## Possible Inquiry Directions

Depending on the subject and evidence, useful directions may include:

- recurring patterns, contrasts and clusters;
- earlier/later forms;
- possible predecessors, lineages or genealogy;
- branching, divergence or specialization;
- combinations, hybrids and recombination of older ideas;
- coexistence, adoption, displacement, persistence and revival;
- cross-domain transfer;
- distributions/prevalence/segment concentration when meaningful;
- constraints, enabling conditions and contextual changes;
- documented influence or creator intent;
- plausible drivers/hypotheses;
- what changed, what stayed stable and what appears genuinely novel;
- other questions surfaced by the investigation.

These are inquiry prompts, not mandatory Result Units.

## Time

Time may be central, secondary or irrelevant.

Entry-level date/period remains Entry metadata at the best supported precision. Landscape time framing is selected only when it helps the current question. It may emerge/refine during inquiry rather than being fixed exhaustively at Target entry.

## Evidence And Epistemic Boundaries

Distinguish:

```text
chronological precedence
documented influence
structural similarity
inferred lineage
causal explanation
```

One does not automatically prove another.

For any material evolutionary or causal interpretation, preserve whether it is:

- directly supported/documented;
- strongly supported interpretation;
- plausible hypothesis;
- unresolved/competing interpretation.

A neat genealogy is not required when the evidence supports only partial relationships.

## Corpus / Evidence Base

Use the smallest evidence base sufficient for the useful claim.

A Landscape may use:

- selected Bank Entries;
- cross-Bank visible Entries;
- Tags / Relations / Analysis Records;
- materialized Entry lists/query inputs when exact reproduction or later comparison is materially useful;
- ad hoc search/filter/query;
- external Sources/Evidence when Bank coverage is insufficient.

Do not imply that Bank-visible material represents a broader population unless evidence supports that scope.

## Depth / Stop Rule

Continue investigation while another reasonable step is likely to materially improve the current understanding.

Stop or synthesize when:

- available Sources no longer support stronger interpretation;
- further breadth adds examples but little new understanding;
- unresolved uncertainty cannot be reduced proportionally;
- the current purpose is already served;
- deeper work would belong to a new concern/Target.

## Findings And Current Understanding

Landscape results may contain:

- supported observations;
- relationships and comparisons;
- partial chronology/evolutionary interpretation;
- distributions when useful;
- hypotheses and competing explanations;
- turning points or persistent branches when supported;
- material unknowns/open questions;
- limits of the available evidence.

Population words such as `common`, `dominant`, `emerging`, `declining`, `saturated` require evidence appropriate to that scope and normally remain Landscape Findings rather than intrinsic Entry Tags.

## Snapshot

When persistence is useful, a retained Landscape Snapshot has a stable Snapshot ID and `Created At`. Record `As Of` / observed period when temporal framing matters and optional `Supersedes` when a newer Snapshot intentionally succeeds an older retained understanding. Retain enough scope/evidence/inquiry context for the understanding to remain historically interpretable. Exact reproduction is not required by default; when it is materially useful, retain the needed corpus membership, query inputs and/or relevant taxonomy assumptions with that Snapshot. Do not require every possible analytical axis, frozen copies of all vocabulary, or a complete historical tree.
