# Current-Owner Evolution Impact Projection Contract

Status: active shared SDS semantic contract
Purpose: own the reusable reverse navigation/revalidation semantics used by current realized Feature, Scenario, Screen, Domain, Slice and Shared owners when concrete unrealized Evolution Steps materially affect them.

## Ownership Boundary

```text
TM-EVOLUTION-STEP / Target Owner Body
→ owns future target/impact meaning

current realized natural owner / owner-local Evolution Impact Unit
→ owns reverse navigation + revalidation projection only

concrete owner Target Module
→ owns the local Unit identity and what counts as material impact on that owner family

Steps Map
→ may route/project Step identity/planning position
```

The reverse projection never becomes a second future roadmap or Target Owner Body.

## Inclusion Contract

For a current realized owner, include every **concrete unrealized Evolution Step** whose current Step-side planning contains material meaning about that owner.

```text
Selected
Probable / candidate
conditional
deferred
branch-local concrete Step
→ all may be referenced when material
```

Selection is not the threshold. Exclude vague future ideas that have no concrete Step identity/material owner impact.

## Projection Content

Each projected entry is proportional and may contain only what helps navigation/revalidation, for example:

```text
Step reference / identity
truthful planning position / enclosing candidate authority when material
concise affected-owner impact summary
revalidation trigger / pressure when material
```

Projection depth must not exceed Step-side meaning already resolved. Do not copy Target Owner Bodies, future Requirements, selected discovery bodies or literal realization detail into the current owner.

The projection may be stored, generated or derived according to representation needs.

## Temporal Boundary

```text
concrete Step remains unrealized + materially affects current owner
→ active future-impact projection

Step realized/materialized
→ remove it from active future-impact projection
→ retain only independently useful lineage/navigation if needed
```

A candidate/Probable/conditional/deferred Step remains visibly non-selected. A selected Step remains unrealized future meaning until realization/materialization.

## Consumer Contract

Feature/Scenario/Screen/Domain/Slice/Shared Target Modules declare their owner-local reverse-impact Unit and specialize only:

- the Unit identity;
- the owner-local affected surface/materiality test;
- local handoff/revalidation consumers.

They reference this shared contract for inclusion threshold, planning-position truthfulness, projection depth and no-copy temporal semantics instead of restating those rules.
