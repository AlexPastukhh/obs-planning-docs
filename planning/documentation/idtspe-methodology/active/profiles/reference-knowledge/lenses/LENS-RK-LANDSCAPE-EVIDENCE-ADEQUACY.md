# Landscape Evidence Adequacy

Lens ID: `LENS-RK-LANDSCAPE-EVIDENCE-ADEQUACY`  
Activation: `FREQUENT_CONDITIONAL`  
Status: active profile Lens

## Purpose / Evaluation Objective

Evaluate whether the available evidence supports the scope and strength of a proposed Landscape observation, comparison, evolutionary interpretation, distribution claim, lineage hypothesis or causal explanation.

## Applicability Gate

Apply whenever a Landscape interpretation may overreach its evidence, especially for broad population claims, temporal/evolutionary narratives, inferred genealogy/influence or causal explanation.

## Target Inputs / Evidence

Landscape concern/scope, selected evidence base, Entry metadata, Tags/Relations/Analysis, external Sources, chronology, provenance, proposed Findings/interpretations and material missing evidence.

## Analysis Surface / Supported Operations

Surface: evidence supporting one or more Landscape interpretations.

Supported operations: `ANALYZE`, `CHECK`, `CHALLENGE`, `REFINE`.

## Evaluation Workflow

Check only dimensions relevant to the proposed claim, including as applicable:

- scope/population alignment;
- source quality and provenance;
- chronology precision;
- sample/corpus coverage and selection bias;
- whether similarity is being mistaken for influence;
- whether precedence is being mistaken for causality;
- vocabulary/identity drift;
- missing competing explanations;
- whether further investigation is likely to materially improve confidence.

## Broad Discussion / Key Points

May explain uncertainty, competing interpretations, missing evidence and what kind of additional Source would materially improve understanding.

## Findings / Outputs

May recommend:

- keep interpretation at current strength;
- narrow scope;
- label as hypothesis;
- preserve several plausible interpretations;
- expand/rebalance evidence;
- revalidate Entry identity/vocabulary first;
- stop investigation because stronger inference is not justified/proportionate.

## Typical Consumers

[Landscape Analysis](../target-modules/TM-RK-50-LANDSCAPE-ANALYSIS.md#tm-rk-50-landscape-analysis) and any downstream consumer relying on retained Landscape understanding.

## Guards / Anti-patterns

A large corpus is not automatically representative. Chronology is not influence. Similarity is not lineage. A coherent story is not automatically better than an honest partial/uncertain model.

## Composition

Compose with applicable Domain analysis Lenses, Entry Identity / Duplication when identity topology affects interpretation, and Tag Quality / Taxonomy when classification drift matters.

## Escalation / Revalidation

Material evidence/identity/vocabulary gaps route through Core Finding Disposition to the natural owner. Recheck when material Sources, Entry identity, vocabulary or the Landscape concern changes.

## Artifact / File Implications

`NONE / NO_DISTINCT_SUPPORTING_ARTIFACT`. Evidence-adequacy Findings remain Core state or integrate into the owning Landscape result.
